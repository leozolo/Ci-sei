# Surface: Club dei clubbers member app

Scope and mode: the whole member app (board, event, calendar, activity, profile, sign-in). Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: 40+ friends in Rome; see what is on and who is going, answer with one tap, organise in the event chat.
Constraints: dark by default (use scene: phones at night, outdoors and in dark venues); posters shown whole; bottom tab bar; light motion; RSVP keeps green / yellow / red from the brief, each state also labelled; Italian copy; symbol undecided, so the mark stays swappable from one file.
Memorable moment: putting yourself on the list.
Unresolved: final symbol.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every event is a door list: the friends who are in come first, ticked in marker with their +1, and the poster follows whole beneath. Refuses the category default of full-bleed posters under gradient overlays with an attendee count at the bottom.

OWN-WORLD: Night-blue ground, never neutral black; chalk-white type; marker green, yellow and red as the list's inks for in, maybe and no. Names, counts and titles in a narrow uppercase grotesk; reading text in the phone's system face. Rows and hairlines rather than cards; drawn icons; no emoji, glass or glow.

STORY: In seconds a member sees which friends are going where this week, puts themselves on a list with one tap, and sorts lifts and tickets in that event's chat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with wordmark and search; "Sei in lista" rows with green ticks; "Questo weekend" door cards, faces and "12 in lista" first, then title, then the poster whole; filters; tab bar Bacheca, Calendario, Proponi, Attività, Profilo. Signature: tapping "Mettimi in lista" writes your name into the list and a marker tick draws itself beside it.

FORM: The door guest list, position 1 on my ordered list (offered as the pick beside the assigned position 4, the running order; the user chose it). seed:6314cc2c376e6fa9
